## PART II — WHAT THE RECORD ESTABLISHES

1. **Sampling was by the SEZ formation, not SIIB.** The six Test Memos of 05.02.2026 (Nos. 02–07 of 2026 (NDR-FTWZ)) issue from the **Office of the Development Commissioner, MEPZ SEZ**, and the CRCL reports were sent back **to the Development Commissioner, MEPZ SEZ** on 25.02.2026. This is the SOP route. It is the opposite of what happened on 07.08.2026 (ACC) and 10.08.2026 (FTWZ), where SIIB officers drew samples themselves.

2. **The goods were tested and answered the declaration.** CRCL reported on **06.02.2026** — within one day of drawal — "It has characteristics of Blister Copper", with copper content in the 96–97% range, and expressly reported the **gold content** of each sample. The very question SIIB is now investigating (precious metal in blister copper) was put to CRCL in the Test Memos of 05.02.2026 and was answered.

3. **The reports reached the Department on 25.02.2026 but the goods were not released for another month.** The hold after 26.02.2026 was **not** on account of testing; the AO's own e-mails record it was on directions of **DRI, New Delhi**, and the importer was passed to **SIIB, Custom House, Chennai-III Commissionerate** (seasiib.chn@gov.in) on 04.03.2026.

4. **The importer asked for the seizure memo/panchanama on 04.03.2026 and never got one.** Neither a seizure memo, mahazar, sampling memo nor a Section 144 receipt appears anywhere in the record. The Department did not deny seizure and did not produce a document; it simply redirected the importer to another formation.

5. **The Section 110A request was made on 04.03.2026 and was never decided.** It was neither granted nor rejected; the goods were released only when OOC issued on the ICEGATE system.

6. **The episode ended in release, not in any adverse finding.** Final OOC copy and eGatepass issued on **20.03.2026 07:30** (the company's own letters say OOC 24.03.2026 — the two dates need reconciling from the OOC copy itself). No show-cause notice, no seizure, no confiscation.

7. **The chain-of-custody and disclosure failures were already complained of.** The reports were handed over only on **07.04.2026**, more than two months after they were issued and after the goods had been cleared; the remnant samples were returned only then.

8. **The 26.02.2026 visit mirrors 10.08.2026.** Officers came without prior intimation, took copies of the B/E, invoices and records, photographed the goods and questioned staff, **without disclosing their identity or department** — exactly the pattern complained of on 10.08.2026, except that this time a summons was left.

## PART III — HOW THIS IS USED IN THE WRITS

- **Repeated/parallel investigation (Instruction No. 27/2024-Customs, 01.11.2024).** The same unit, the same commodity (blister copper under CTH 74020010), the same supplier line (Gemtec Jewellery FZE), and the same issue (precious metal content) were investigated in Feb–Mar 2026 on DRI (New Delhi) directions and routed through SIIB, Chennai-III, and were closed by OOC. Six months later two more formations — ACC SIIB (07.08.2026) and SIIB Chennai-III Preventive (10.08.2026) — reopened the identical question. Para 2.3 of the Instruction required a DIGIT/DIN check "in terms of the existing investigations related to the same entity and also if an investigation on the same issue exists" before initiating; para 2.1 required the Commissioner's prior approval.
- **The Department speaks with one voice** — *Birla Corporation*, (2005) 6 SCC 95; *Damodar J. Malpani*; *ITC*.
- **The prescribed sampling route is the SEZ/Authorised Officer route, and the Department itself said so.** The AO's e-mail of 24.02.2026 09:01 states the samples "were drawn and forwarded to CRCL, Chennai **as per the prescribed procedure**". That is the benchmark against which the twin SIIB drawals of 07.08.2026 and 10.08.2026 — with no memo, no receipt and no chain of custody — must be judged.
- **CRCL can turn a blister-copper sample around in one day** (drawn 05.02.2026, reported 06.02.2026). The present hold pending test therefore cannot be justified on laboratory delay — Circular 01/2011-Cus., para 4 and 4(d) (continued detention beyond 3 days to be brought to the Commissioner's notice) and *B.V. Leathers*.
- **An unanswered s.110A request plus an unproduced seizure memo is precisely the *Exim Incorporation* fact-pattern** — "First illegality is they have detained the goods without affecting seizure." The February episode shows this is the formation's settled practice, not an isolated lapse.
