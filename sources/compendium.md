## PART IV — WHAT ACTUALLY CREATES THE "Z" TYPE BILL OF ENTRY

There is **no notification that prescribes a "Z-type Bill of Entry" as such.** "Z" is a document-type code in the Customs Automated System (ICES/ICEGATE). Its legal chain is:

1. **Rule 29, SEZ Rules, 2006** — the substantive procedure and the only provision that governs the filing; Rule 29(2) makes the assessed/endorsed BoE the permission to move the goods into the Zone. **[Verified]**
2. **Notification No. 36/2018-Customs (N.T.) dated 11.05.2018, G.S.R. 447(E)** — Bill of Entry (Electronic Integrated Declaration and Paperless Processing) Regulations, 2018, made under s.157 read with ss.46–47: the BoE is filed electronically and is deemed filed with self-assessment completed on entry of the declaration on the Customs Automated System. **[Verified]**
3. **Department of Commerce (SEZ Division) letter No. J.16/3/2007-SEZ Vol-V dated 22.06.2024** (on DG Systems, CBIC e-mail dated 19.06.2024) — filing of import/export documents for **all non-IT/ITES SEZs and FTWZs moves to ICEGATE w.e.f. 01.07.2024**; no further filing on the NSDL SEZ-Online system; model Public Notice annexed, "Roll out of Indian Customs EDI System — ICEGATE for Imports and Exports at SEZs". **[Verified]**
4. **Same file No., clarification dated 29.06.2024** — transition; RoDTEP only for ICEGATE filers; SEZ-Online permitted till 15.07.2024; and expressly: *"The 'T' type B/E in ICEGATE meant for DTA clearance from trading/warehousing unit cannot be filed unless there is a corresponding 'Z' type B/E, such details being mandatory."* **[Verified]**
5. **ICEGATE "FAQs on SEZ filing (Part-I)", Query No. 9** — "What are the types of BE available for SEZ in ICEGATE 2.0?" — a **"Z" type Bill of Entry is filed for importing goods from foreign territory to a SEZ unit, and is similar to a Warehouse / Into-Bond Bill of Entry filed at a Customs port**; the same reply deals with IGM and trans-shipment permission for moving cargo to the SEZ from the gateway port. **[Reported — quoted verbatim in the AAAR order in *Panasonic Life Solutions*; obtain a dated printout of the ICEGATE FAQ page as an exhibit]**
6. **"USER MANUAL — SEZ WEB FORMS (ICEGATE)", Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, dated 26.06.2023, Version 1.01** — the official ICEGATE 2.0 manual for the SEZ Bill of Entry web form. **[Verified — full text read; 28 pages]** Its material contents are set out in Part IV-A below.
7. **DG (Systems & Data Management), CBIC advisory — "Process of customs clearance of Imported goods from foreign country to SEZ unit in ICES"** (46 pages, with sample Z, T and M type Bills of Entry and the officer-role screens). **[Verified — full text read]** Its material contents are set out in Part IV-A below.
8. **Other DG Systems/ICEGATE SEZ advisories** — "SEZ Goods Movement on ICEGATE 2.0", "SEZ DTA Procurement", "ONLINE PAYMENT OF CUSTOMS DUTY (SEZ to DTA Bill of Entry Transactions)", "SEZ Registration Amendment", "Advisory for SEZ Registration on ICEGATE 2.0" — all hosted in the ICEGATE Advisory index on the SEZ websites. **[Reported — index read and verified; download the individual advisories that are actually relied upon]**
9. **ICES Advisory No. 13/2026 (DG Systems) and the corresponding Custom House Public Notices** on automated SEZ ↔ Customs Bonded Warehouse movement — the 'Z' type BE is the document by which a SEZ unit receives goods, and a 'W' type BE with the 'Z' BE reference is used for movement to a bonded warehouse; importer type "T" for as-is clearance and "M" for post-manufacture clearance. **[Reported — obtain the advisory and the Chennai Public Notice]**
10. **Commissionerate Public Notices** implementing the ICEGATE roll-out at SEZs, and the Chennai Air Cargo Complex Public Notice on **movement of SEZ/FTWZ cargo out of ACC** — this names the officer competent to permit the movement, which is not SIIB. **[Not traced online — to be obtained from the Commissionerate; important for Writ No. 1]**

Codes: **Z** = import into SEZ/FTWZ; **T** = DTA sale from a trading/warehousing unit; **M** = DTA sale from a manufacturing unit; **V** = inter-SEZ transfer; **W** = warehouse (bonded) movement.

**The negative proposition to plead:** none of these instruments — Rule 29, the 2018 Regulations, the letters of 22.06.2024 and 29.06.2024, the ICEGATE advisories or any Public Notice — confers on any officer a power to withhold, suspend or keep in abeyance a Bill of Entry that has already been assessed, or to stop the movement of the goods under it. The only statutory routes are s.17(4)/(5), s.18 and s.110.

## PART IV-A — THE ICEGATE 2.0 SEZ WEB FORMS MANUAL AND THE DG SYSTEMS ADVISORY: WHAT THEY ACTUALLY PRESCRIBE

Both documents have now been read in full and both are official CBIC documents. Together they are the best available proof of the **prescribed sequence** for a Z-type consignment, and they show that what was done at the Air Cargo Complex on 07.08.2026 has no place in that sequence.

### 1. The manual — "User Manual, SEZ Web Forms (ICEGATE)", CBIC, 26.06.2023, Version 1.01 **[Verified]**

| Clause / place | What it says | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| §3 (Overview) | With the addition of the SEZ type Bill of Entry, the **Bill of Entry message format remains the same**, but the data validations change with the Z and T type, ICEGATE validating structure and fields and **ICES doing the data validation** | The "Z" is a validation/document code within one and the same statutory Bill of Entry — it is not a lesser or informal document, and it is not a species of clearance outside the Act |
| §5.3 | On selecting "SEZ Bill of Entry" only two types are available: **"Z type BOE :- SEZ BE (FTA-SEZ)"** and **"T type BOE :- SEZ (DTA Sales – Trading)"** | Confirms the code meanings pleaded |
| §5.3.2, Segment-1 | The first segment of the Z-type form is **"General and Warehouse Details"** — IEC, importer name and address, **Custom House Code where the BE is getting filed**, BE type, broker code, country of origin | The form is a warehousing document filed at the SEZ Custom House code, not at the gateway |
| §5.3.2, Segments 4 and 5 | **Bond details** (bond number, bond code, Custom port of registration; multiple bonds possible) and, in the alternative, **certificate in lieu of bond** | The revenue on a Z-type BoE is secured by the Unit's registered bond — the answer to any "interest of revenue" objection to release |
| §5.3.2, Segments 6–7 | IGM particulars (IGM no./date, inward date, AWB/BL, HAWB/HBL, packages, gross weight) and container/seal details | The system already holds the package and seal record against which any drawal of samples must be reconciled |

### 2. The DG Systems advisory — "Process of customs clearance of Imported goods from foreign country to SEZ unit in ICES" **[Verified]**

**(a) The prescribed sequence, in the Department's own words:**

> "For imported goods, complete process in ICES would be as follows: Filing of IGM (by shipping line) → Filing of bill of entry (by importer) → Assessment of bill of entry (by officer through APR & ACL roles) → **Transhipment permission at gateway port/airport → Registration of goods at SEZ (by officer through AAI role) → Examination/inspection of goods (by officer through INS role)** → Out of charge (by officer through SUP role)"

This is the single most useful sentence found so far. On the Department's own process document, **examination and inspection of the goods come after the goods have been moved to the Zone and registered there** — exactly as the MEPZ SOP for Blister Copper requires examination and sampling "at the time of warehousing" and by the Authorised Officer. The events of 07.08.2026 at the Air Cargo Complex — drawal of samples at the gateway, before trans-shipment and before registration at the Zone — invert the prescribed sequence and were done by an officer holding none of the ICES roles (APR/ACL, AAI, INS, SUP) to which the function is assigned.

**(b) The nature of the document:**

> "For importing goods from foreign territory to the SEZ unit, 'Z' type bill of entry has been developed in ICES. **The bill of entry format is similar to a warehouse bill of entry filed at a customs port.**"

Confirms from a primary CBIC source what the ICEGATE FAQ says — the goods are warehoused, not cleared for home consumption, and no duty arises at this stage.

**(c) Salient features of the Z-type BoE that help us:**

| Feature (verbatim or close paraphrase) | Use in the writ |
| --- | --- |
| "A 'Z' type bill of entry **can be filed at any SEZ location against a line of the IGM irrespective of the port of destination** declared in the IGM" (example given: IGM destination INSSA1, Z-type BoE filed at **INMAA6 for a unit at MEPZ SEZ**) | The jurisdiction over the consignment is the SEZ location's, chosen by the filing; the gateway formation is not the assessing or examining authority. The illustration in the advisory is the very port code of MEPZ SEZ |
| "**Transhipment permission** for moving the cargo to SEZ from gateway port would be provided at gateway port/airport, if the destination mentioned in IGM is either SEZ location or gateway port" | Movement out of the Air Cargo Complex is a defined, system-driven permission — a ministerial act on the assessed BoE, not a discretion of an intelligence wing |
| "Transhipment permission can be generated **only when there is sufficient balance available in the bond** submitted by the SEZ unit. An amount equal to duty value would be debited at the time of approval of transhipment" | The duty is secured on the bond before the goods move; there is nothing left to protect by holding the goods |
| "Bill of entry can be filed when there is sufficient balance in the Bond … an amount equal to **duty forgone** amount would be debited in the system" | Same point at the filing stage |
| "For consignments where **no foreign exchange is involved at the time of import (where foreign supplier is keeping his goods in the SEZ unit)**, a dummy AD code 'NFEI' has been created" | The Department's own system expressly contemplates our exact model — a foreign supplier (M/s Gemtec Jewellery FZE) keeping its goods in an Indian FTWZ unit. This answers any insinuation that the arrangement is itself irregular |
| "**There is no first check option available for 'Z' type Bill of entry**" | Decisive on the sampling. "First check" is the very procedure of examining/testing goods before assessment. The system does not permit it for a Z-type BoE at all. What was done on 07.08.2026 was a first check by another name, for which there is no provision |
| "Assessment and examination of bill of entry would **depend upon RMS risk treatment** of bill of entry" | Any examination must be RMS-directed and performed in the INS role; no RMS instruction has been shown |
| "This type of bill of entry **would not be sent to PGA (Partner Government Agency) for NOC**" … whereas "**all the PGA's requirements would be applicable at the time of DTA clearance**" (T type) | Quality/compliance checks on the goods belong to the DTA-clearance stage, not the warehousing stage — which is precisely why the SOP defers OOC to the test report and does not authorise a gateway hold |
| "ICES is **maintaining a ledger** of all Z type of bills of entry and goods cleared against T type bill of entry are updated in the ledger … the balance quantity … has to exist in the ledger" | The goods cannot be diverted; every gram is accounted for in the system before any DTA clearance is possible. There is no risk that release into the Zone defeats the investigation |
| "An option has been provided to the SEZ officers to **credit the bond amount**, if the liability associated with the bill of entry is complete" | The bond mechanics are the designed security; provisional release on bond is the ordinary course, not an indulgence |

**(d) The officer roles.** The advisory reproduces the screens for **Assessment** (APR and ACL roles, with the officer's SSO ID mapped to the HSN Assessment Group before a BoE can even enter his queue), **Registration of goods**, **Examination of goods** and **Out of charge** (SUP role) — each "to be done by an officer" in the assigned role.

> **The ground this yields:** the Customs Automated System allots each act on a Z-type Bill of Entry to a named role — APR/ACL for assessment, AAI for registration at the Zone, INS for examination, SUP for out of charge — and an officer of the intelligence wing holds none of them. An assessment made in the system by the officer in the APR/ACL role, on a Bill of Entry from which the duty forgone has already been debited to the Unit's bond, cannot be set at nought by an officer outside the system who has neither passed an order under s.17(4)/(5) or s.18 nor effected a seizure under s.110. And the drawal of samples at the gateway on 07.08.2026, before trans-shipment and before registration of the goods at the Zone, reverses the sequence the Department itself prescribes and amounts to a "first check" which, on the Department's own document, is **not available at all** for a Z-type Bill of Entry.

### B. No power of "detention" outside s.110

| Case | Citation | Holding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ***Jatinder Kumar Sachdeva v. Union of India*** | Delhi HC, 08.12.2016 **[Verified]** | There is **no provision for "detention"** in the Customs Act; the Department cannot, "under the garb of detention", escape the consequences that follow a seizure. |
| ***Exim Incorporation v. Union of India*** | Bombay HC (DB), 09.12.2020 **[Verified]** | DRI directed the port formation to hold assessed cargo and ignored a s.49 request; the **first illegality was detaining without effecting a seizure**. |
| ***Union of India v. Jatin Ahuja*** | SC, Civil Appeal No. 3489 of 2024, 11.09.2025 **[Reported]** | s.110(2) time discipline. |
| ***S.J. Fabrics Pvt. Ltd. v. Union of India*** | Calcutta HC, 19.04.2011 **[Reported]** | Detention/seizure safeguards. |
| ***M/s Om Udyog v. Union of India*** | Punjab & Haryana HC, 14.05.2010 **[Reported]** | Detention without seizure impermissible. |

Caution: the express "no detention power" holdings are **Delhi and Bombay**, not Madras. Do not attribute that formulation to the Madras High Court; use *J.K. Exim* and the s.110/110A discipline for the Madras position.

### C. Customs jurisdiction inside a SEZ — in our favour

| Case | Citation | Holding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ***M/s Meenakshi International v.
